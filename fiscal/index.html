Sources & method. Updated 2026-07-31 15:40 ET. Health scores are statistical readings of each series against its own history (trailing z-scores) or fixed institutional thresholds where the absolute level is intrinsically meaningful (SOFR−IORB, the coupon fuse, interest/receipts); they are descriptive, not predictive, and the composite is a weighted average, not a model of anything. Sources: Treasury Fiscal Data (debt, auctions, TGA, MSPD, MTS, interest expense), FRED/Federal Reserve H.4.1 (balance sheet, reserves, RRP, rates, NFCI, HY OAS), NY Fed markets API (SOFR percentiles, primary-dealer fails), U.S. Treasury daily par yield curve. Curve noise is a Nelson-Siegel RMSE in the spirit of Hu-Pan-Wang (2013) computed on par yields — levels are methodology-specific; only its own history is comparable. Data vintages differ by series (daily to quarterly) and are stamped per metric.
