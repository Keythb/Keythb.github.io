$TLT
On September 20, 2026 we published "The Capex Comeback Is One Line Wide." Part of that note rests on the reading that capital is not leaving, and the note named in advance the result that would make us revise it.

The test used the Treasury International Capital (TIC) data, the US Treasury's monthly count of foreign net purchases of long-term US securities. It would fire if the August and September releases both showed net foreign sales, or if twelve-month net purchases fell below $1.47 trillion. At publication the twelve months to July stood at $1,754.4 billion.

The test did not fire. Both months showed net purchases, not sales: $307.0 billion in August and $186.7 billion in September. Net purchases for the twelve months to September came to $1,865.2 billion, against the $1.47 trillion bar. The twelve months to September 2025 had come to $1,507.6 billion.

The two-release window is now closed, and the note's reading that capital is not leaving stands.
