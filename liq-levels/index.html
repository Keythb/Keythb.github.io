COINGLASS MODEL 3 · 48H HEATMAP · 2026-07-29
Disclosures. Updated 2026-07-29 00:30 PT. Levels are the value-weighted centers of the densest clusters (≥75th percentile, contiguous bins) in Coinglass's Liquidation Heatmap Model 3 at the latest timestamp; clusters below spot are labelled long entries, above spot short entries. Touch probabilities assume driftless geometric Brownian motion at the stated 48h vol — BTC has fat tails, so far-level probabilities skew low by construction; the point of the track record is to measure that gap. Each day's levels and distance-matched thin-liquidity control levels are logged and scored out-of-sample 48h later. The sweep-order test compares the realized touch sequence of each day's levels against a driftless GBM Monte Carlo run on the same level placement at that day's realized vol.
