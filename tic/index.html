Are foreign holders walking away from U.S. Treasuries?
NO — DEMAND IS NORMAL
Foreign buying over the last three months is running at or above its typical pace.
But not for the reason the headline implies. Official reserve managers ran net sales of $50.3B over three months — 6th percentile of their own history — and private buyers more than absorbed it with net purchases of $124.0B. The composition of foreign demand is shifting even where the total is not.
Three-month reported net purchases by all foreign holders: +73.7 $B · 68th percentile of the series’ own history since Mar 1985 · 1 of 4 tripwires triggered.
