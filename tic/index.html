Are foreign holders walking away from U.S. Treasuries?
NOT YET — DEMAND IS SOFT
Foreign buying has slowed to the bottom half of its historical range, but the sector is still absorbing paper.
But not for the reason the headline implies. Official reserve managers ran net sales of $73.7B over three months — 4th percentile of their own history — and private buyers more than absorbed it with net purchases of $95.1B. The composition of foreign demand is shifting even where the total is not.
Three-month reported net purchases by all foreign holders: +21.4 $B · 41st percentile of the series’ own history since Mar 1985 · 1 of 4 tripwires triggered.
