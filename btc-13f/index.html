Caveats

Scope. Form 13F is filed by managers with discretion over $100m or more of 13F securities; everyone smaller, and every direct holder, is invisible here. Positions are as of the quarter end and filed up to 45 days later.
Double counting. A parent and its sub-adviser can both report the same shares. The page flags filers listed as included managers on another cover page (paler bars) and reports a deduped total; the top-20 chart is raw, as the filings are.
Hedged holdings. Trading firms, market makers and authorized participants hold ETF shares against derivatives or inventory; their bars are positions, not views.
Options excluded. Rows with a put/call flag are kept in the data but out of every total.
Amendments. A RESTATEMENT replaces the original; a NEW HOLDINGS amendment adds to it; an amendment whose type cannot be read is treated as a restatement when it carries at least as many holdings as the original.
Classification. Registry-based, not reviewed name by name except where the hand file says so; a bank's registered-adviser arm classifies as an adviser.

Sources & method

13F-HR and 13F-HR/A filings: SEC EDGAR full-text search (efts.sec.gov) for each fund's CUSIP and name within the filing window, then each filing's information table and cover page via EDGAR. Filer type: SEC Form ADV monthly registry (Item 5D regulatory assets by client type, Item 7B), snapshot ia09012026.csv. Trust facts: SEC XBRL companyfacts API per trust. Quarter-end bitcoin price: CoinMetrics PriceUSD 2026-09-30 (UTC day) — fallback until the trusts' 10-Qs print the NAV strike = 83,579.67. Pipeline: poker-assistant/thirteenf_holdings.py · btc_per_share.py · classify_13f.py · build_13f_page.py.
